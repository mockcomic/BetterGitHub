# Aspen mark — generation & provenance

The Aspen app mark (`resources/aspen-icon.svg`) was generated with the
local ComfyUI instance (ComfyUI-Easy-Install, RTX 3090) on 2026-10-30 and
vectorized by hand-tracing the winning render. No credentials or private
connection details are recorded here — only the public ComfyUI graph.

## Pipeline

1. **Round 1 — three prompt variants × 2 samples** (batch 2, 1024×1024,
   euler/simple, 28 steps, cfg 2.5). Scores: warm-white-leaf variants 7–8/10
   (leaf outline too faint for small sizes), green-leaf variant 8/10.
2. **Round 2 — refined prompt, 4 seeds** (batch 1, 1024×1024, 28 steps,
   cfg 3.5 for crisp geometry). Winner: **seed 13337** (9/10 vision QA —
   clean aspen silhouette, exactly three commit nodes, one gold accent, no
   garble). Runners-up kept here as `alt-seed5150.png`, `alt-seed8823.png`,
   `alt-seed24091.png`.
3. **Vectorization**: the winning render's green silhouette was masked
   (flood-filled background), exported as a bitmap, and traced with
   potrace 1.16; the resulting outline was Douglas-Peucker-simplified to 74
   straight segments (deliberate: the flat geometric look), refit into
   512-space, and stored as shared data in `leaf-path.json`. The veins and
   commit nodes are exact primitives drawn over it, not traced.

`aspen-logo-workflow.json` is the API-format graph for the selected render;
re-queue it against ComfyUI `POST /prompt` to reproduce `source-render-seed13337.png`.
Round-1 candidate seeds for the record: 42101, 77312, 99104.
